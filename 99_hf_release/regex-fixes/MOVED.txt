The UltiMed regex fixes live in their own repository:
https://github.com/UltiMed-Org/UltiMed-ASR-FR-v2-regex-fixes

They are lists of rules (drug names, medical terms, hand-written fixes) that rewrite the
misspellings a parakeet model still makes on French medical vocabulary, for example
"veine la faxine" becomes "venlafaxine". They were learned from the errors of the finetune
on this dataset, and also help models that were not finetuned. Each list ships both as
regular expressions and as lookup tables for apps whose regex engine has no lookbehind.
The README there explains how they were built and how to apply them, and the repository
also holds the scripts that build them.
